Rules

  • Get 3 in a row to win.
  • Each player keeps at most 3 marks.
  • When you place your 4th mark, your oldest one disappears.
  • Your oldest mark blinks when it is about to go.

Can this game be won?

Yes. I solved every reachable position (66,682 of them) with a full retrograde analysis. Under the standard rule, the first player has a forced win.

  • Open on an edge (top, left, right or bottom middle). Every reply by O loses with perfect play.
  • Opening in the center or a corner only leads to a draw.
  • The fastest forced win takes 13 plies: 7 moves by X, 6 by O.
  • Of all positions, 45,709 are wins for the player to move, 13,388 are losses, and 7,585 are draws.

First move value for X: DWDWDWDWD W = forced win, D = draw.

The exact rule matters. If your oldest mark leaves before you place, so you can put the new mark on the same cell, the game turns back into a draw, like classic tic-tac-toe. Switch the fade rule in the panel and turn on solver hints to see the difference.

Classic tic-tac-toe is a draw because the board fills up. Here the board never fills, so the side to move keeps the initiative and can build double threats that cannot both be blocked.
