Fade rule

The standard rule gives X a forced win. The other one turns the game back into a draw.

Rules

Four lines. The fourth one changes everything.

  1. 01Get three of your marks in a row to win.
  2. 02Each player keeps at most three marks on the board.
  3. 03When you place your fourth mark, your oldest one disappears.
  4. 04Your oldest mark blinks on your turn, so you see what is about to go.

Can it be won?

Yes. Every reachable position was solved with a full retrograde analysis. With the standard rule, the first player has a forced win.
